## Direction contract
THESIS: "El escrito bien hecho": the page has the composure of a carefully drafted Argentine judicial writing (papel, margen, firma, "Será justicia"), but speaks the client's problem in plain words. Refuses the stock law-firm hero (gavel stock photo + "Excelencia jurídica" + 3 icon cards).
OWN-WORLD: deep walnut brown fields (#2a1c13 / #3b281d), papel cream #f7f3ee, brand beige #e6d8cb, bronze #a67c52 hairlines and seals; Cormorant Garamond display set like the logo's engraved caps, Jost for reading; the F&Y scales isotipo as a seal; hairline rules, no cards-as-structure, no shadows except on photos.
STORY: Visitor sees two real lawyers and "Salta" in the first glance, picks their situation in plain words, gets what the firm does for it plus which papers to bring, and taps WhatsApp to a named lawyer with the message pre-written.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full walnut field. Left 7/12: headline Cormorant ~5rem cream, one-line support, WhatsApp primary (cream button, chooser popover for the lawyer) + "Reservar turno" link, modality row. Right 5/12: two portrait plates staggered, names set beneath like signatures. Mobile: headline, CTAs, portraits side by side below.
FORM: signature interaction "Contanos qué te pasa" situation selector with crossfade/blur result panel and pre-filled WhatsApp; closing "Será justicia." contact block. Seed key: n/a (pinned world).
